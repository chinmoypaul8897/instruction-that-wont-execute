## TASK

### 1. Downloads land in `data/raw/` — never tracked
`data/raw/` is git-ignored. **Extract-then-freeze:** nothing enters `data/` except extracted artifacts. A single ECFR title XML is tens of MB; fifty are ~2.3 GB, against a 50 MB submission cap. **If you find yourself about to `git add` an XML, stop.**

### 2. Extract `<EDNOTE>` structurally
Parse the XML. Do not regex the raw text. For each `<EDNOTE>` record: title, part, section, whether it sits **inside** a `<SECTION>` block, and its full text.

### 3. Filter to codification-defect notes
Those containing **`"could not be incorporated"`**. Record the count that matched and the count that did not — both, per title.

### 4. Resolve the FR citation
Every defect note should carry its own FR citation (e.g. `89 FR 54360`). Extract it. **Report the fraction that carry one** — resolution is deterministic when they do, and a search step when they don't.

### 5. Report the pool as counts AND as a rate
Per title and in total:

| | |
|---|---|
| `<EDNOTE>` elements | total |
| of which codification-defect | count **and % of EDNOTEs** |
| of which section-level (not appendix/part) | count |
| of which carry an FR citation | count |
| **defect notes per title** | the rate — this is what projects to the full corpus |

**Reference measurements from 9 titles (12, 20, 21, 24, 26, 40, 42, 45, 49):** 903 EDNOTEs → **44** defect notes, **44/44** carrying an FR citation, 38/44 section-level. Sanity-check against these. **A large deviation is a finding, not an error — report it with the numbers, do not tune toward the reference.**

Expected full pool: **150–250** defect notes, ~130–210 section-level. *(An earlier eCFR search-API figure of 92 is believed to undercount by ~2.3×. Republish your govinfo number beside it rather than replacing it.)*

### 6. Publish the exclusion ladder
Every filtering step with its count, committed as `docs/evidence/ch01-pool/exclusion-ladder.md`. A reader must be able to follow total → usable without guessing.

### 7. Freeze what you extracted
`data/ednotes/` — the extracted records only, with a SHA-256 manifest and a `refetch.py` that reproduces them from govinfo. Print `du -sh data/` and `git ls-files | wc -l` at the end.
