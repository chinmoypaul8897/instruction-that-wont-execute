  LAYER 3 - WHAT THE OVERRIDES MEAN. `->EXECUTES` is the Step 2.5 cross-check
  firing: the resolver said the target is ABSENT and the model ruled the
  instruction executes anyway, having checked `siblings` against the section
  text. That is the shape that recovers Q21's manufactured false defects, and
  the difference between A1 and A1-iter1 on this row is the SKILL's whole
  contribution made visible.

  AVAILABILITY IS NOT USE, AND USE IS NOT AGREEMENT. An arm can hold the tool
  and never call it; call it and ignore the answer; or call it, disagree, and
  be RIGHT to. Only the third layer separates a capability that was shipped
  from one the agent actually exercised.

REP-TO-REP STABILITY AT TEMPERATURE 0 - and why it bounds the ablations
==============================================================================

  B0-agent         3 reps   per-rep accuracy ['0.6585', '0.6585', '0.6585']
                   spread 0.0 pp   max pairwise disagreement 0.0% of items
                     rep1 vs rep2: 0 of 82 items differ

                     rep2 vs rep3: 0 of 82 items differ
  A1               3 reps   per-rep accuracy ['0.7195', '0.6707', '0.7195']
                   spread 4.9 pp   max pairwise disagreement 14.6% of items
                     rep1 vs rep2: 12 of 82 items differ
                     rep1 vs rep3: 10 of 82 items differ
                     rep2 vs rep3: 9 of 82 items differ

  A TEMPERATURE-0 TOOL-USING ARM IS NOT DETERMINISTIC. The checkpoint's three
  B0-agent reps were identical (0.6585 x3) because that arm makes ONE call per
  item. A1 runs an agentic loop, and the loop varies in how many tool calls it
  makes and in what order - so each turn is sampled from a context the previous
  turn shaped. Determinism at temperature 0 is a property of a SINGLE call, not
  of a multi-turn agent, and this is the measurement of the difference.

  WHAT THIS COSTS THE ABLATIONS, stated plainly. A1-iter1, A1-minus-tool and
  B0prime are ONE REP each (GOOD.md section 8; ruling R-01 item 2). Any gap
  between them smaller than the spread above is INSIDE THE RUN-TO-RUN NOISE and
  must not be read as an effect. The ablation ordering is reported with that
  caveat attached rather than presented as if the numbers were exact.

  hard rule 9 - DETERMINISM - is not violated by this. It binds the SCORER and
  the RESOLVER, both of which are pure and byte-reproducible; a sampled model is
  not in its scope. What would violate the rule is claiming a model arm is
  reproducible when it is not, which is why this section exists.
