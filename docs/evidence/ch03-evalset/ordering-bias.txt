RETRACTION
====================================================================================

  The figure published as '32/38, exact p = 0.000024' is WITHDRAWN. It was
  computed by an uncommitted inline snippet that RECONSTRUCTED the pairing
  from the frozen items file by re-matching on (frdoc, instruction_count),
  rather than by running the rule. The round-2 reviewer could not reproduce
  it and was right not to be able to.

  The correct pre-fix figure, from running the pre-fix rule:
      36/50 negatives sort before their positive,  exact p = 0.002602
  The correct shipped figure:
      25/50 negatives sort before their positive,  exact p = 1.000000

  The DIRECTION and the CONCLUSION are unchanged - the pre-fix rule is
  strongly biased and the shipped rule is not - but the published number was
  wrong and no script backed it. Both defects are the build session's.

  NOTE ON THE PAIR COUNTS. These are the pairing stage only, BEFORE the
  point-in-time text lookup and the leakage test remove items, so they are
  larger than the frozen n. The frozen eval set is 41 pairs / 82 items.
